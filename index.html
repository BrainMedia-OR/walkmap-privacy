WalkMap Privacy Policy

Android app “WalkMap” (package gop.craterlake.walkmap), version 1.6.0 and later
Effective date: November 9, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version
  • WalkMap is a private, invitation-only tool for volunteers of Crater Lake GOP. It is not for the general public and not for children.
  • Volunteers sign in with a Microsoft work account that the campaign set up for them. The app then reads the campaign’s walk list from the campaign’s private Microsoft OneDrive/SharePoint folder and writes the volunteer’s canvassing results back to that same folder.
  • The app uses your phone’s location only while the app is open on screen (to show where you are, find the next door, and place yard signs/posters). It does not track you in the background.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not share it with anyone outside the campaign except Microsoft, which runs the sign-in and file storage for us.
  • Voter information is used only for political campaign outreach, never for commercial purposes.
  • Questions or deletion requests: michael.brainard@craterlake.gop.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

WalkMap is provided by Crater Lake GOP (“we”, “us”, “the campaign”) for its volunteers in Oregon. We are responsible for the information described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions, access or deletion requests: michael.brainard@craterlake.gop

2. Who the app is for

WalkMap is a door-to-door canvassing (“walking”) tool for invited, adult volunteers of Crater Lake GOP. It is invitation-only: the app itself contains no voter data. Voter data reaches a phone only after a campaign administrator has given a volunteer a Microsoft account in the campaign’s organization and access to the campaign’s private data folder (or, as a fallback, gives the volunteer a walk-list file to open in the app). Anyone else who installs the app sees no voter data. The app is not intended for the general public and not intended for anyone under 18.

3. What information the app uses

a. About you (the volunteer)

  • Account email / user name. When you sign in with Microsoft, the app receives your account user name (your email address). It is used to decide what you are allowed to see, and it is written next to the results you record (for example, “recorded by jane@…”) and in the names of the files the app saves for you in the campaign folder (for example, Marks_jane.csv). The app never sees or stores your password; Microsoft handles the sign-in.
  • Your name (optional). If you are not signed in, you can type a name in the app’s settings; it is used to label your marks instead of an email.
  • Precise location. With your permission, the app uses your phone’s GPS while the app is open to show your position on the map, find the nearest next door, order a walking route, and place a yard sign or poster where you are standing. Your live location is not uploaded or tracked. The only location that leaves your phone is the map position of a yard sign or poster you choose to record (which may come from your GPS at that moment), and the map position of a house you manually correct (“Teleport”).
  • What you record. Door results (for example “Not home”, “Will vote”), “voted” marks, notes, voter tags, voter-registration follow-up, door flags (for example “No soliciting”, “Unsafe”), yard signs and posters (location, size, candidate, dates, notes), and a log of when you tapped “Call” or “Text” for a voter (date, time, number). Each record includes the time and your email or name.
  • Access requests. If you are a captain allowed to invite people, the email addresses and areas you request for new volunteers are saved to the campaign folder for an administrator to review.

b. About voters (people who are not app users)

The app displays a walk list that the campaign prepares and places in its private folder. It is built from Oregon voter registration lists and campaign records and may include, for each voter: name, home address and map location, age, gender, registered party, registration status, a campaign-estimated party-leaning score, phone numbers and do-not-call flags, voting history and whether/when a ballot was returned, election districts, and (in some lists) a church affiliation. Volunteers may add notes, tags (which can describe a voter’s views or how a visit went) and results about these voters. This information is sensitive, and we treat it that way (see sections 8–10).

c. What the app does not collect

  • No advertising ID, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no tracking pixels or cookies.
  • No access to your phone’s contacts, photos, camera, microphone, text messages or call history. The Call and Text buttons simply open your phone’s own dialer or messaging app with the number filled in; the app does not place calls or send messages, and it cannot see them.
  • No payment information.

5. How we use information

  • To show each volunteer the doors they are assigned, record what happened at each door, and share results with the rest of the campaign team so people do not knock on the same door twice.
  • To control who can see which voters (section 8).
  • To plan and measure the campaign’s outreach (progress dashboards and walk reports) and to track yard signs and posters.

We do not use any of this information for advertising, we do not build profiles of volunteers, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

6. Where information is stored

  • On your phone. The walk list, your marks and your team’s synced results are stored in the app’s private storage so the app works without a signal. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data. Results you record offline are uploaded the next time you have a connection.
  • In the campaign’s Microsoft account. The walk list and your uploaded results are kept in a private OneDrive/SharePoint folder that belongs to Crater Lake GOP in Microsoft 365. Microsoft stores this data for us under its own security and privacy terms.
  • If you export. “Export my marks” creates a file of your results and hands it to the app you choose (for example email or Drive). What happens to it after that is controlled by you and that app.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Within the campaign. Results you upload are visible to other authorized campaign volunteers and administrators who have access to the campaign folder.
  • Microsoft processes sign-in (Microsoft Entra ID) and stores the campaign folder (OneDrive/SharePoint, accessed through Microsoft Graph). As part of sign-in, Microsoft’s library also sends Microsoft standard technical details such as app and library version and device operating system and model. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement. On some phones, Microsoft’s sign-in library may use Google Play services to offer passkey or saved-credential sign-in if your organization enables it.
  • Map tile servers. To draw maps, the app downloads map images from OpenStreetMap servers and from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS National Map). Like any website, these servers see your phone’s internet address and which map squares are requested. No voter data, names or account information is ever sent to them.
  • Apps you open. If you tap “Walk directions”, the destination point is passed to Google Maps (or your browser). If you tap the Oregon online voter registration link, the Oregon Secretary of State website opens in your browser. Those services follow their own privacy policies.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the safety of volunteers or others.

8. Who can see voter data (access control)

  • Only people the campaign has given a Microsoft account and access to the private campaign folder can download any data. The folder is never shared with “anyone with the link”.
  • The campaign keeps a role list (Admin, Captain, User). Admins see all areas; Captains see their assigned House District(s); Users see only the precincts or turfs assigned to them. A signed-in person who is not on the role list sees no voters. Voters outside your assigned area are removed from your phone at sign-in and every refresh.
  • When a volunteer leaves, administrators remove their access to the folder and the campaign’s Microsoft organization, which stops further downloads. Volunteers are asked to use Erase all data on this phone or uninstall the app when they are done (section 12).

9. Voter records and Oregon law

Voter information in WalkMap comes from voter lists that political parties and campaigns may lawfully obtain, and from the campaign’s own outreach records. We use it only for political campaign and election-related outreach (such as reminding supporters to vote, voter registration help and volunteer recruitment). Oregon law (ORS 247.955) prohibits using voter list information for commercial purposes; we do not, and volunteers may not copy, sell or use it for anything else. If you are a voter and want to know whether we hold information about you, want it corrected, or do not want to be contacted, email michael.brainard@craterlake.gop and we will honor do-not-contact requests in our walk lists.

10. Security

  • All network connections the app makes (Microsoft sign-in, Microsoft Graph, map servers) use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Sign-in is handled by Microsoft; the app never sees your password. Access to the folder uses your own account permissions, not public links.
  • Data on the phone is kept in app-private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox and your phone’s device encryption and screen lock. Please use a screen lock on any phone that has WalkMap.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security problem affecting your information, we will notify affected people as required by law.

11. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you choose Erase all data on this phone or uninstall the app. Voters who drop off the campaign’s list, or fall outside your assigned area, are removed from your phone on the next refresh.
  • In the campaign folder: only as long as the campaign needs it for outreach in the current and upcoming election cycles. Administrators then delete it.
  • Microsoft sign-in records are kept by Microsoft under the campaign’s Microsoft 365 settings and Microsoft’s policies.

12. Deleting your data

  • On the phone: in WalkMap open the menu (⋮) and choose Erase all data on this phone (deletes voters, your marks, notes, signs, posters and call/text log), and Sync settings / sign-in → Sign out. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on the phone.
  • In the campaign folder and your account: email michael.brainard@craterlake.gop from the address you use in WalkMap (or tell us which address) and ask us to delete your volunteer data and/or your campaign Microsoft account. We will confirm your request and delete or anonymize your files, your entries in the role list and your account within 30 days, unless we must keep something to meet a legal obligation (we will tell you if so). Results you recorded about voters may be kept by the campaign with your name or email removed.
  • Your WalkMap account is a campaign Microsoft account created by our administrators, not inside the app, so deletion is done by our administrators on request.

13. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings.
  • You can turn off “Sync marks with team” in the app settings; your marks then stay on your phone only.
  • You can ask us at michael.brainard@craterlake.gop for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

14. Training mode

WalkMap includes a Training mode for practice. It uses a separate set of made-up demo voters (for example “Demo Voter 01” on “Demo Lane”). In Training mode nothing is synced, uploaded, imported or exported, and the practice data is discarded when you leave Training mode.

15. Children

WalkMap is not directed to children and is only for adult volunteers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 18 as an app user). If you believe a child has used the app, contact michael.brainard@craterlake.gop and we will delete their information.

16. Not a government app

WalkMap is an independent volunteer tool of Crater Lake GOP. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Oregon Secretary of State, any county clerk or elections office, or any other government agency. It does not register voters or handle ballots; for official election information visit sos.oregon.gov/voting or your county elections office.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. We will post the new version at this same web address with a new “Last updated” date, and for significant changes we will tell volunteers through the campaign or in the app.
